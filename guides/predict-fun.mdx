Use Bravado to execute trades, analyze wallets and stream live order books on Predict.fun.

- [Trade API](/markets/predict-fun/trade-api): route requests with `venue: predictfun`.
- [Trader Data](/markets/predict-fun/data-api): profiles, PnL, positions, trades, categories and leaderboards under `/trader-analytics/predict/`.
- [Order-book WebSockets](/api/market-data/venue-order-books): connect to `/stream/predictfun/market` and subscribe by outcome token ID.

Your Trader Data account needs `analytics.read` and Predict.fun enabled. Contact Bravado for evaluation access and integration assistance; no trading permission is required for analytics.

Copytrade, Combos, PMWAS statements, tax reports and entity rollups are not currently available on Predict.fun. See [venue coverage](/markets/predict-fun/overview).
