Prediction market books are thin. A market order for size does not get filled at the price you saw; it walks the book and you pay the average. This guide covers the two tools for working size in gradually, and when each is the right one.

## The problem, concretely

Say the best ask is `0.62` with 200 shares behind it, then `0.64` with 300, then `0.68`. A market buy for 1,000 shares takes all three levels and your average lands near `0.66`, not `0.62`. On a thin book you moved the price yourself, and anyone watching saw it happen.

Two ways to avoid that:

| | TWAP | Iceberg |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Splits by | Time | Displayed size |
| Takes or makes | Takes liquidity in clips | Rests passively, post-only |
| Fill certainty | Higher, it keeps buying | Lower, waits to be hit |
| Best when | You must get filled within a window | You would rather wait than pay the spread |

## TWAP: spread it over a window

Break a budget into clips executed at intervals:

```json
{
  "type": "TWAP",
  "symbol": "71321045679252212594626385532706912750332728571942532289631379312455583992646",
  "side": "BUY",
  "budget_usdc": "2000",
  "execution": {
    "duration_sec": 3600,
    "interval_sec": 60,
    "price_tolerance_pct": 2,
    "randomize_pct": 10
  }
}
```

That spends \$2,000 over an hour in roughly 60 clips.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="duration_sec and interval_sec">
    Seconds, not minutes. `interval_sec` has a **minimum of 10**. Clip count is roughly `duration_sec / interval_sec`, so the pair determines how big each clip is: too few clips and each one moves the market, too many and the strategy runs longer than your edge lasts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="price_tolerance_pct">
    A percent, `0` to `100`. Clips are skipped when the price has moved beyond this from the strategy's reference. This is your protection against filling into a spike. Setting it too tight means the strategy silently does nothing in a moving market.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="randomize_pct">
    A percent that jitters clip timing and size, so execution does not appear as an obvious metronome. Useful when predictability itself is the cost.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

The response gives you a `record_id`, not an `order_id`:

```json
{ "record_id": "rec_twap_abc123", "status": "ACTIVE", "type": "TWAP" }
```

## Iceberg: show a slice, hide the rest

Rest a large order while only exposing a clip at a time:

```json
{
  "type": "ICEBERG",
  "symbol": "...",
  "side": "BUY",
  "price": "0.60",
  "size": "5000",
  "execution": { "clip_size": "100" }
}
```

<Warning>
  **Iceberg slices are post-only.** For a buy, the price must be at or below the current best bid. Price it above and the slice is rejected with `order crosses book`, because it would take liquidity rather than provide it. Iceberg cannot be used to fill aggressively.
</Warning>

That constraint is the whole trade-off: an Iceberg waits to be hit. If the market runs away from your price, you do not get filled at all.

## Monitoring

Managed strategies do not appear on the open orders endpoint. This trips people up constantly:

```bash
curl https://bravado-api-k7kaq.ondigitalocean.app/v2/trade/strategies \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $BRAVADO_API_KEY"
```

An Iceberg is visible in two places at once: the parent as a `record_id` on `/strategies`, and whichever slice is currently resting as a child `order_id` on `/v2/trade/orders/open`.

<Note>
  `PENDING` means the engine has accepted the strategy and is waiting for conditions to be met. It is not a rejection.
</Note>

## Cancelling

```bash
curl -X DELETE https://bravado-api-k7kaq.ondigitalocean.app/v2/trade/strategies/rec_twap_abc123 \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $BRAVADO_API_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: $(uuidgen)"
```

Cancelling stops future clips. Anything already filled is yours, so read the strategy record for filled size before deciding what to do with the position.

<Warning>
  `POST /v2/trade/orders/cancel-all` does **not** touch managed strategies. It clears CLOB orders only. A TWAP keeps running after it. Cancel strategies individually.
</Warning>

## A practical combination

For a large position where you want most of it passively but need a floor on completion:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Rest the bulk as an Iceberg">
    Place 70% at or below the touch. If the market comes to you, this is the cheap fill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a TWAP for the remainder">
    Put the other 30% on a duration that matches your deadline, with a `price_tolerance_pct` you can live with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch both on /strategies">
    Poll for filled size across the two records rather than assuming either completes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel the Iceberg once the TWAP finishes">
    Otherwise it keeps resting and you end up with more size than intended.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Venue constraints that cause rejections

These come from the venue, not Bravado:

- Resting orders need **at least 5 shares**, which sets a floor on `clip_size`.
- Market orders need **\$1 minimum** notional, which sets a floor on TWAP clip size via `budget_usdc / clip count`.
- Prices are decimal probabilities from `0.001` to `0.999`.
