Two reads: every withdrawal of the user, or one withdrawal by the `withdrawal_id` that [`POST /v2/trade/perps/withdrawals`](/api/trade/perps/withdraw) answered. Scope `perps.read`.

## List withdrawals

```text
GET https://partner-api.bravadotrade.com/v2/trade/perps/withdrawals
```

### Query Parameters

<ParamField query="cursor" type="string">
  Opaque. Pass back the previous page's `next_cursor`.
</ParamField>

### Response

The user's withdrawals. When there are more, pass the page's `next_cursor` back as `cursor`.

After a `504` on a withdrawal, list withdrawals before acting again: the request may have acted.

## Get one withdrawal

```text
GET https://partner-api.bravadotrade.com/v2/trade/perps/withdrawals/{withdrawal_id}
```

### Path Parameters

<ParamField path="withdrawal_id" type="string" required>
  The `withdrawal_id` the `POST` answered, a UUID.
</ParamField>

### Response

The withdrawal, with its `state` and `tx_hash`. Poll until `state` is `confirmed`.

- When the venue pays out promptly, the withdrawal shows `confirmed` with its `tx_hash` within about 10 seconds of the request, so poll every few seconds at first.
- Bravado reports `confirmed` only once the pUSD transfer to the deposit wallet is verified on chain; `tx_hash` can appear while `state` is still `pending`.
- States are `pending`, `confirmed`, `failed`, `rejected` and `unknown`.

### Errors

| Status | When |
| --- | --- |
| `404 Not Found` | `NOT_FOUND`: unknown id, or one that belongs to another user |
