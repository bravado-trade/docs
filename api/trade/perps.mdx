Polymarket Perps is Polymarket's perpetual futures venue. The Perps endpoints let you place and manage perpetual futures orders for your users through the Trade API you already integrate. Bravado routes each order to the venue in the user's own Polymarket Perps account, where it is matched. **Bravado is not the counterparty** to any trade and does not hold the collateral: pUSD moves from the user's deposit wallet to their venue account and back.

<Warning>
  **Enterprise plan only, switched on by Bravado.** Perps is available on the Enterprise plan and on no self-serve plan. Being on Enterprise does not turn it on by itself: ask your Bravado integration manager to enable it for your organisation. Until it is enabled, every `/v2/trade/perps/*` path answers `403 INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE`, naming the missing scope, even with a correctly signed request. Once enabled, your key needs **`perps.read`** for reads and **`trade.perps`** for everything that changes state.
</Warning>

## What stays the same

Perps is part of the Trade API, not a separate product:

- **Same base URL, keys and signing.** Paths live under `/v2/trade/perps/`, signed with the [HMAC headers](/authentication) you already send.
- **Same users.** Perps acts for one integration user at a time: send a user key, or your master key with `X-Partner-User-Id`, exactly as on the other user routes. See [sub-accounts](/guides/white-label-sub-accounts).
- **Same `Idempotency-Key`.** Required on every `POST`, honoured on `PATCH` and `DELETE`.
- **Same deposit wallet.** Deposits are funded from the user's deposit wallet, and withdrawals are always paid back to it.

What is different:

- **Deposit-wallet (V2) users only.** A user still on a V1 Safe gets `409 USER_PROVISIONING_PENDING` with `reason: "v2_wallet_required"` on enrolment and on deposits. Migrate the user first.
- **The perps balance is separate.** pUSD you deposit to Perps leaves the deposit wallet. It cannot back prediction-market orders or be sent out with [`/v2/trade/withdraw`](/api/trade/withdraw) until you withdraw it from Perps back to the deposit wallet.
- **Amounts, prices and quantities are decimal strings.** Quantities are in the base asset (for example BTC), not in pUSD.

  Perps features roll out in stages. </Note>

## The flow

```
readiness → accounts → deposits → leverage → orders → reduce_only close → withdrawals
```

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check readiness">
    [`GET /v2/trade/perps/readiness`](/api/trade/perps/readiness) tells you the next step for this user. Orders need `ready` or `credential_expiring`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enrol the user">
    [`POST /v2/trade/perps/accounts`](/api/trade/perps/enrol) takes your attestation of where the end user is. Bravado supplies the user's wallet; you send only the attestation. A user in a blocked jurisdiction gets `403 GEO_BLOCKED` and is never enrolled. An attestation older than 180 days makes opens answer `409 USER_PROVISIONING_PENDING` with `reason: "attestation_stale"`: enrol again with a fresh attestation.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deposit pUSD">
    [`POST /v2/trade/perps/deposits`](/api/trade/perps/deposit) moves pUSD from the user's deposit wallet to their venue account. Poll [`GET /v2/trade/perps/deposits/{deposit_id}`](/api/trade/perps/deposits#get-one-deposit) until `state` is `credited`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set leverage">
    [`PATCH /v2/trade/perps/leverage`](/api/trade/perps/leverage) sets leverage **per instrument, for the whole account**. It is not an order field.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place orders">
    [`POST /v2/trade/perps/orders`](/api/trade/perps/place-order) answers `201` when the venue answered, or `202 pending_confirmation` when the venue may or may not have the order. On a `202`, **do not send it again, and never under a new key**: poll [`GET /v2/trade/perps/orders/{intent_id}`](/api/trade/perps/orders#get-one-order).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Close with reduce_only">
    To close, send the opposite side with `reduce_only: true`. A close is exempt from `min_notional`, and a partial close may not leave less than `min_notional` open. See [Close a position](/api/trade/perps/place-order#close-a-position).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Withdraw">
    [`POST /v2/trade/perps/withdrawals`](/api/trade/perps/withdraw) moves pUSD from the venue account back to the user's deposit wallet. Poll [`GET /v2/trade/perps/withdrawals/{withdrawal_id}`](/api/trade/perps/withdrawals#get-one-withdrawal) until `state` is `confirmed`; when the venue pays out promptly, that is within about 10 seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Limits

Every order rule is checked **before** the order reaches the venue. Each input is on `GET /v2/trade/perps/instruments`; read the values from there rather than hard-coding them, because they can change. A refusal frees your `Idempotency-Key` so you can fix the request and retry with the same key. When Bravado enforced the rule, the refusal names it in `details.rule`.

| Rule | Refusal | `details.rule` |
|---|---|---|
| `price` has at most `price_decimals` decimal places | `400 INVALID_PRICE` | `price_decimals` |
| A non-integer `price` has at most **5 significant figures**. At a BTC mark near 83,000, that means an integer price (`81478`, not `81478.5`) | `400 INVALID_PRICE` | `price_significant_figures` |
| `\|price − mark_price\| ≤ mark_price × price_bounds`. `price_bounds` is a fraction: `"0.05"` would be 5%. Leave room, because the mark moves | `400 INVALID_PRICE` | `price_bounds` |
| `quantity` has at most `quantity_decimals` decimal places | `400 INVALID_SIZE` | `quantity_decimals` |
| `quantity × (price, or the mark without one)` is at least `min_notional`. Closes are exempt; see [Close a position](/api/trade/perps/place-order#close-a-position) | `400 INVALID_SIZE` | `min_notional` |
| A partial close leaves at least `min_notional` open, valued at the mark | `400 INVALID_SIZE` | `reduce_only_remainder_below_min_notional` |
| Notional is at most `max_limit_notional` (with a price) or `max_market_notional` (without) | `400 INVALID_SIZE` | `max_limit_notional`, `max_market_notional` |
| A deposit is at least the minimum deposit. A deposit with more than 6 decimal places is refused by request validation, with no `details` | `400 INVALID_BODY` | `min_deposit` |

Your integration also has a policy that Bravado sets with you when Perps is enabled:

| Policy | Refusal |
|---|---|
| Notional cap per order, and on the user's total notional | `409 NOTIONAL_CAP_EXCEEDED` |
| Maximum leverage | `409 LEVERAGE_CAP_EXCEEDED` |
| Instrument allowlist | `403 INSTRUMENT_NOT_ALLOWED` |
| Reduce-only mode: opens are refused, and an order against the position goes reduce-only, capped at the position size (`reduce_only_forced`, `quantity_capped` on the `201`) | `403 REDUCE_ONLY_MODE` |
| Deposits or withdrawals switched off | `403 OPERATION_DISABLED` (`reason` says which) |

## Errors

Errors use the Trade API envelope, plus two optional fields:

- **`reason`**, a lower-case token, when one code has several next steps.
- **`details`**, an object. It holds `rule`, a lower-case token, when Bravado names the rule it enforced (see [Limits](#limits)); this can be on any code. Only beside `rule: "reduce_only_remainder_below_min_notional"` does it also hold `position_size`, `remainder` and `min_notional`, as decimal strings. No other field appears in it.

Any refusal may come without `details`: a refusal from request validation (a malformed field, a missing header) has none. Check that it is there before reading it, and read only the fields you know.

The `reason` values below are the ones you will meet most. **The list is open**: Bravado may send others, and a code may come with no `reason` at all. On a `reason` you do not know, read `GET /v2/trade/perps/readiness` before acting, rather than treating it as fatal.

| `reason` | Code | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| `v2_wallet_required` | `409 USER_PROVISIONING_PENDING` | Migrate the user to a deposit wallet |
| `not_enrolled` | `409 USER_PROVISIONING_PENDING` | [Enrol the user](/api/trade/perps/enrol) |
| `needs_deposit` | `409 USER_PROVISIONING_PENDING` | The account has no collateral: [deposit](/api/trade/perps/deposit). Reduce-only orders are not refused for this |
| `attestation_stale` | `409 USER_PROVISIONING_PENDING` | Enrol again with a fresh attestation, under a new `Idempotency-Key` |
| `credential_expired` | `409 USER_PROVISIONING_PENDING` | The venue credential is being renewed, and every signed call waits for it, closes included. Retry later with the same key |
| `enrolment_in_progress` | `409 USER_PROVISIONING_PENDING`, `Retry-After: 5` | Another enrolment for this user is running. **The key is kept**, so a retry with the same key replays this `409`. Wait, then read readiness |
| `withdrawable_unreadable` | `409 USER_PROVISIONING_PENDING` | `"max"` needs a readable account; send an explicit amount |
| `close_only` | `403 INSTRUMENT_NOT_ALLOWED` | The venue made the instrument close-only; only reduce-only orders are accepted |
| `no_position` | `409 POSITION_OR_ORDERS_EXIST` | TP/SL needs an open position on the instrument |

```json reason
  "reason": "v2_wallet_required",

```


### When to retry, and with which key

**Refused before the venue: retry with the same key.** These codes mean nothing reached the venue. Fix the cause, or wait for it to clear, and send the request again with the **same** `Idempotency-Key`:

`GEO_BLOCKED`, `NOTIONAL_CAP_EXCEEDED`, `LEVERAGE_CAP_EXCEEDED`, `INSTRUMENT_NOT_ALLOWED`, `REDUCE_ONLY_MODE`, `PERPS_HALTED`, `VENUE_CANCEL_ONLY`, `ACCOUNT_IN_LIQUIDATION`, `PRICE_SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`, `LOCAL_RATE_BUDGET_EXCEEDED`, `CLOCK_SKEW_REFUSED`, `OPERATION_DISABLED`, `PERPS_CAPABILITY_UNAVAILABLE`, `INVALID_PRICE`, `INVALID_SIZE`, `INVALID_BODY`, and `USER_PROVISIONING_PENDING` from the readiness check.

Some of these codes can also come back after the venue was reached, and then the key is kept. `VENUE_CANCEL_ONLY` can be the venue's own answer. `INVALID_SIZE` can be the venue's refusal of a close. `USER_PROVISIONING_PENDING` with `reason: "enrolment_in_progress"` also keeps the key. In these cases a retry with the same key replays the stored answer (`X-Idempotent-Replay: true`).

**Answered by the venue: the key is kept.** Any other answer is stored and replayed for the same key (`X-Idempotent-Replay: true`). Read the order, position or transfer state first, and use a new key only once you know the first request did not act.

A `409` with `PERPS_HALTED`, `VENUE_CANCEL_ONLY`, `CLOCK_SKEW_REFUSED` or `PERPS_CAPABILITY_UNAVAILABLE` means "try later", not a conflict to abandon. Wait for `Retry-After` when it is sent, then repeat with the same key. Generic HTTP clients rarely retry a `409` on their own, so handle these codes explicitly.

<Warning>
  **A `504` is an unknown outcome, never a failure.** Bravado's hosting edge turns every `502` and `503` into an HTML `504`, so the JSON body never reaches you. The `X-Bravado-Error-Code` header usually does. Treat any `504` as "the request may have acted":

  - **Do not send it again under a new key.** Retry with the **same** `Idempotency-Key`: Bravado either replays the stored answer or acts once.
  - **For an order, look it up first** at `GET /v2/trade/perps/orders/ik:<your key>`. While it answers `pending_confirmation`, keep polling; `rejected` with `venue_status: "not_placed"` means nothing was placed.
  - **For a deposit or withdrawal**, list `GET /v2/trade/perps/deposits` or `/withdrawals` before acting again.
</Warning>

### Perps error codes

| Code | HTTP | Meaning | Key |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GEO_BLOCKED` | 403 | The attested jurisdiction cannot trade perps; the user is never enrolled | Freed |
| `INSTRUMENT_NOT_ALLOWED` | 403 | The instrument is outside your allowlist. Also: `reason: "close_only"`, an open on an instrument the venue made close-only, and `reason: "no_policy"`, Perps is not configured for your integration yet | Freed |
| `REDUCE_ONLY_MODE` | 403 | Your integration is in reduce-only mode; only closes are accepted | Freed |
| `OPERATION_DISABLED` | 403 | Your policy does not allow this deposit or withdrawal | Freed |
| `NOTIONAL_CAP_EXCEEDED` | 409 | Above your notional cap | Freed |
| `LEVERAGE_CAP_EXCEEDED` | 409 | Above your leverage cap | Freed |
| `ACCOUNT_IN_LIQUIDATION` | 409 | The venue is liquidating the account; opens are refused | Freed |
| `PERPS_HALTED` | 409 | Bravado has halted perps opens; try later | Freed |
| `VENUE_CANCEL_ONLY` | 409 | The venue is cancel-only (for example, maintenance); `Retry-After: 60` | Freed before the venue, kept after |
| `CLOCK_SKEW_REFUSED` | 409 | Clock disagreement with the venue; nothing was signed. `Retry-After: 5` | Freed |
| `PERPS_CAPABILITY_UNAVAILABLE` | 409 | This feature is not live yet; `reason` names it. `Retry-After: 60` | Freed |
| `USER_PROVISIONING_PENDING` | 409 | The user is not ready; `reason` says why (see the list above, which is open) | Freed before the venue, kept after and on `enrolment_in_progress` |
| `INVALID_PRICE`, `INVALID_SIZE`, `INVALID_BODY` | 400 | The request breaks an order rule (see [Limits](#limits)); `details.rule` names it when Bravado enforced it. A venue refusal of a close is also `INVALID_SIZE` | Freed before the venue, kept after |
| `LOCAL_RATE_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` | 429 | Bravado's shared venue budget refused before sending; `Retry-After` | Freed |
| `VENUE_RATE_LIMITED` | 429 | The venue throttled the request; `Retry-After` | Kept |
| `INSUFFICIENT_MARGIN` | 422 | Not enough margin for the order | Kept |
| `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE` | 422 | Not enough pUSD to deposit, or a withdrawal above `withdrawable` | Kept |
| `INVALID_LEVERAGE` | 422 | The venue refused the leverage for the position's risk tier | Kept |
| `REDUCE_ONLY_VIOLATION` | 422 | A `reduce_only` order would increase or flip the position | Kept |
| `MODIFY_WOULD_CROSS` | 422 | The modified price would cross the book | Kept |
| `MARKET_REJECTED` | 422 | The instrument is close-only or settled | Kept |
| `POSITION_OR_ORDERS_EXIST` | 409 | The margin mode cannot change while a position or orders exist. With `reason: "no_position"`, the opposite: TP/SL was set with no position | Kept |
| `ORDER_TERMINAL` | 409 | The order is already filled or cancelled | Kept |
| `ORDER_IN_FLIGHT` | 409 | The order is changing at the venue; `Retry-After: 1` | Kept |
| `MODIFY_REJECTED` | 409 | The venue refused the modify; `reason` says why | Kept |
| `ACTIVE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` | 409 | Too many open orders | Kept |
| `NOT_FOUND` | 404 | Unknown id, or one that belongs to another user | n/a |
| `PRICE_SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE` | 503 → HTML 504 | The mark is stale; nothing was sent | Freed |
| `VENUE_UNAVAILABLE` | 503 → HTML 504 | Bravado could not reach the venue; the outcome of a write is unknown | Kept |
| `UPSTREAM_TIMEOUT` | 502 → HTML 504 | A non-order write whose outcome is unknown | Kept |

When Bravado halts perps, opens, leverage increases and removing isolated margin (a negative `delta`) answer `409 PERPS_HALTED`. Cancels, reduce-only orders and closes, TP/SL cancels, leverage decreases, deposits, withdrawals and reads keep working, so your users can always exit.

<Tip>
  Quote `request_id` (or the `X-Request-Id` header on a `504`) when you contact Bravado about a perps request.
</Tip>
