## Research with wallet and market data

Use [Trader Data](/products/data-api) for wallet trade history, positions and PnL series on Polymarket and Predict.fun. Use [live order books](/api/market-data/venue-order-books) to collect forward-looking market observations.

| Research input | Interface |
| --- | --- |
| Wallet trades | `GET /trader-analytics/traders/{address}/trades` |
| Wallet positions | `GET /trader-analytics/traders/{address}/positions` |
| Wallet PnL | `GET /trader-analytics/traders/{address}/pnl` |
| Trader discovery | `GET /trader-analytics/leaderboard` |

For Predict.fun, insert `/predict` after `/trader-analytics`. Sign requests using [Data API authentication](/products/data-api#authentication).

Positions describe current/open or closed holdings; this endpoint is not an arbitrary historical timestamp reconstruction API. `/trader-analytics/trades` is wallet-scoped activity, not an unrestricted global historical tape.

## Historical market datasets

For historical L2, OHLCV, open-interest series or Parquet delivery, see [historical data coverage](/api/market-data/historical-data). We can scope a dataset around your markets and date range, including construction of additional research features from available sources.

Agree coverage, gaps, time resolution and delivery dates with Bravado before relying on a dataset for backtesting. Wallet history, sampled prices and full market-state replay have different coverage.

## Accounting and numerics

Read the response's accounting disclosures. Full PMWAS statements and reconciliation are available for processed Polymarket wallets; tax-report fallbacks have different coverage. See [accounting coverage](/products/data-api#accounting-coverage).

Parse decimal strings with arbitrary-precision arithmetic. Follow each endpoint's [pagination contract](/reference/pagination) and preserve returned cursors when resuming extraction.
