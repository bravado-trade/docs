## Why stream through Bravado

The venue's own socket is public, so this is not about access. It is about not rebuilding the same plumbing every prediction-market application needs.

| | Direct venue WebSocket | Bravado Market Data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connections | One socket per market or asset | One socket, many assets multiplexed server-side |
| Trade enrichment | Fetch trader profiles client-side | Name and wallet address already on every trade frame |
| Cold start | Feed begins empty, you wait for the next event | Recent-history snapshot delivered on connect |
| Fan-out | Every client opens its own upstream | Upstream shared and ref-counted across all subscribers |
| Surface | Separate integration per venue | Same interface across supported venues |

## Ultra-low-latency: the mempool feed

For latency-sensitive consumers — market makers, execution engines, signal — Bravado
operates a **mempool-sourced fills feed** in addition to the standard trade feed. Fills
are decoded directly from the Polygon public mempool and delivered **~1–2 s before the
venue's own real-time feed reports them** (measured against Polymarket RTDS: earlier on
~100% of trades, ~1.5–2 s median). Each fill carries:

- The full **pending → mined → settled** lifecycle — including the negative states
  (**reverted / dropped / replaced**), so you learn when a trade *didn't* land, not just
  when it did.
- The **controlling wallet (owner EOA)** behind the trading proxy, inline on every fill —
  not just the proxy address.
- Exact on-chain economics from the settlement calldata, before the trade is confirmed.

<Info>
  The mempool feed is a **partner tier** — token-gated and provisioned during onboarding.
  The standard [Live Trades](/api/market-data/trade-feed) feed uses a WebSockets credential and mirrors
  the venue's own push feed. See the [Mempool Fills feed](/api/market-data/mempool-feed) — contact Bravado to enable it.
</Info>

## What you can build

- **Live tickers and tapes.** Render a running feed of fills across the market, no polling.
- **Depth and price displays.** Drive an order-book widget or a best bid/ask readout straight from the socket.
- **Signal and discovery.** Watch flow across every market and react to it in realtime.
- **Enriched activity.** Every trade arrives with the trader's wallet, so you can cross-reference [Trader Data](/products/data-api) (PnL, positions, history) for the wallets you see trading.

## Historical data and custom delivery

Wallet trade history and PnL are available through [Trader Data](/products/data-api). REST price history and bounded volume series are described in [historical data coverage](/api/market-data/historical-data).

For historical L2 events, Parquet extracts, OHLCV, open-interest series or a market catalog integration, contact us with your markets and date range. We can scope delivery from available archives and develop additional datasets. Coverage and delivery dates are agreed per dataset; these are not unrestricted self-service history endpoints.

## Health and diagnostics

Each feed exposes a plain HTTP status endpoint you can poll from a health check or dashboard:

<CodeGroup>

```bash Trade feed
curl https://partner-api.bravadotrade.com/trade-feed/status
```

```bash Order book feed
curl https://partner-api.bravadotrade.com/orderbook-feed/status
```

</CodeGroup>

The trade-feed status reports whether the upstream source is connected, how many trades it has emitted, the size of the recent buffer, and the current connected-client count.
