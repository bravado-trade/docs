Polymarket markets do not settle themselves. When a market's event concludes, the outcome has to be reported on-chain and accepted before holders can redeem winning shares. Polymarket uses [UMA](https://uma.xyz)'s Optimistic Oracle (V2) for that step, and the Bravado UMA API exposes the resolution lifecycle as both **queryable data** (REST) and a **realtime stream** (WebSocket).

<Info>
  UMA resolution is served from `partner-api.bravadotrade.com`, signed with [HMAC authentication](/authentication). The **REST** routes are part of the
  [Data API](/products/data-api) and take your Data API credential (`analytics.read`); each successful response costs 1 credit. The **WebSocket** is part of
  [Market Data](/products/market-data) and takes your Market Data credential (`stream.read`) on an active paid plan. You do not need a separate UMA key.
  Coverage today is Polymarket.
</Info>

## Choose your interface

The **REST** API is a [Data API](/products/data-api) surface (`analytics.read`, 1 credit per response) and is split into two pages — the live window and all history. The **WebSocket** is a separate [Market Data](/products/market-data) surface (`stream.read`, paid plan). They do not share a credential, and neither is a product of its own.

| You want to… | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Read what is resolving right now, at the lowest latency | [REST — live window](/api/analytics/uma-live) — `/uma/question`, `/uma/market`, `/uma/recent` |
| Look up the resolution of one question or market, over all history | [REST — history](/api/analytics/uma-history#get-/uma/history/question) — `/uma/history/question` or `/uma/history/market` |
| Read questions that changed in a time range, with paging | [REST — history](/api/analytics/uma-history#get-/uma/history/recent) — `/uma/history/recent` |
| Receive new proposals, disputes and settlements as they occur | [WebSocket](/api/market-data/uma-websocket) — `/uma/ws` |

## Coverage and history

Coverage is Polymarket's UMA resolutions: requests from Polymarket's UMA adapters for `YES_OR_NO_QUERY` markets, including NegRisk markets.

- **[History](/api/analytics/uma-history)** (`/uma/history/*`) reads the complete record of oracle events, with `from` / `to` ranges, cursor paging and `resolution_source` provenance. A question that was re-requested returns its latest lifecycle and says how many there were.
- **[Live window](/api/analytics/uma-live)** (`/uma/question`, `/uma/market`, `/uma/recent`, and the WebSocket) reflects what the realtime feed currently holds: about 7 days for settled questions and 14 days for open ones. A `404` there means the record is not retained; it does not prove the question never existed.

A market whose oracle request cannot be found is reported as `resolution_source: "unknown"`, never as an empty or unsettled market. Consuming the live stream does not backfill earlier events; read [`/uma/history/recent`](/api/analytics/uma-history#get-/uma/history/recent) for those.

## The resolution lifecycle

The captured record can contain a proposal, an optional dispute and a final settlement.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Proposal">
    A proposer posts an outcome to the oracle with a bond. This is the `proposed` stage; `answer` carries the proposed result. A proposal observed before mining carries `seen_pending: true` and is provisional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Challenge window">
    The proposal stays open for a fixed liveness period. If nobody disputes it, it becomes eligible to settle on the proposed answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dispute (optional)">
    Any party may dispute within the window by posting their own bond (`disputed` stage). A disputed request escalates to UMA's token-holder vote rather than settling on the proposer's word.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settlement">
    The final answer is written on-chain (`settled` stage, with the `payout`). Winning outcome tokens become redeemable at \$1; losing tokens go to \$0.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A market whose event has visibly concluded is not necessarily settled. Always confirm `settled: true` before treating a position as realized — an undisputed proposal can still be disputed until its challenge window closes.
</Warning>

## Identifiers

Every lifecycle record carries two ids:

- **`question_id`** — `keccak256(ancillaryData)`, the UMA adapter's question id. This is the stable, exact key for a resolution.
- **`condition_id`** — the CTF condition id used everywhere else in Bravado to identify the market. Use it to join resolution state to positions and trades. On `/uma/history/market` it is always the real id; on the other routes it is derived from the question and is **not reliable for NegRisk markets** (see `resolution_source`).

The `answer` field classifies the oracle's `int256` price: `yes`, `no`, `50-50`, `too-early`, or `other`; `price_raw` preserves the exact value.

## Build around final settlement

- Treat a concluded event and a redeemable position as different states. Confirm `settled: true` before presenting a resolution as final.
- Allow for disputes: a proposed answer can change before final settlement.
- Redeem positions only after settlement through the [Trade API](/api/trade/positions).
